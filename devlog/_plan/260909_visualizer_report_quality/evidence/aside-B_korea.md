## 1. Findings (Structured Rules with Direct Evidence)

This section synthesizes statutory and institutional rules governing professional reporting in South Korea across financial investment firms and public/governmental institutions.

### 1.1 Securities Equity Research Regulations (KOFIA)

Securities research reports in South Korea are strictly regulated by the **Korea Financial Investment Association (금융투자협회, KOFIA)** under the *Regulations on Financial Investment Companies' Business and Operations* (금융투자회사의 영업 및 업무에 관한 규정, Part 2, Chapter 1, Section 3 "Research and Analysis Materials", Articles 2-25 through 2-33-2).

1. **Mandatory Analyst Certification (Article 2-27, Paragraph 1):**
   - The producing research analyst must certify in the document that the report was prepared with due diligence, reflecting their honest and independent judgment without external interference or improper pressure.
   - *Direct Quote:* "금융투자분석사는 조사분석자료를 타인의 부당한 압력이나 간섭 없이 본인의 의견을 정확하게 반영하여 신의성실하게 작성한 경우 그 사실을 조사분석자료에 명시하여야 한다." (KOFIA Regulation Art. 2-27, Para. 1).
2. **Conflict of Interest & Shareholding Disclosures (Article 2-29, Paragraph 2 & Article 2-32):**
   - Disclosures must explicitly note whether the securities firm holds 1% or more of the covered issuer's outstanding equity shares, whether an affiliate relationship exists, whether the broker acted as lead manager or liquidity provider within specified timeframes, and whether the analyst or spouse holds financial interests exceeding 3,000,000 KRW in the covered equity.
   - *Direct Quote:* "금융투자자료를 공표하거나 특정인에게 제공하는 경우 자신과의 이해 관계를 조사분석자료에 명시하여야 한다." (KOFIA Regulation Art. 2-29, Para. 2).
3. **Target Price Disparity & Historical Distribution Disclosure (Article 2-33, Paragraph 3 & 5):**
   - Securities reports on listed equities that present investment ratings or target prices must state the definition of the investment rating, provide a 2-year history of rating and target price changes, display a comparative chart of target price versus actual market price, and calculate the price disparity rate (괴리율: maximum/minimum and daily average disparity).
   - *Direct Quote:* "조사분석자료에 제시된 목표가격과 ... 최고주가 ... 또는 최저주가 ... 및 일 평균주가와의 괴리율" (KOFIA Regulation Art. 2-33, Para. 3, Item 3).
   - In addition, brokers must disclose their 1-year historical distribution of ratings across 3 tiers (Buy, Hold/Neutral, Sell) and update this quarterly.
4. **Pre-publication and Front-Running Prohibitions (Article 2-28 & Article 2-31):**
   - Pre-publication leaks to third parties, institutional clients, or internal trading desks without compliance clearance are illegal. The report must state: "본 조사분석자료는 기관투자가 등 제3자에게 사전 제공된 사실이 없습니다."
   - Analysts may not trade stocks in their coverage sector for personal accounts (Art. 2-31, Para. 2).

### 1.2 Public Policy & Economic Research Institutes (KDI, BOK, KIET)

Public policy research reports produced by national think tanks such as the **Korea Development Institute (KDI)**, **Bank of Korea (BOK)**, and **Korea Institute for Industrial Economics & Trade (KIET)** adhere to academic rigor combined with public accountability standards:

1. **Structural Monograph Protocol:**
   - Formal policy studies follow a standardized sequence: Series Cover → Title Page → Foreword/Publisher's Note (발간사) → Executive Summary (요약) → Main Body (Chapters / Sections) → References (참고문헌) → Appendices (부록) → English Abstract (ABSTRACT).
2. **Institutional Disclaimer vs. Author Responsibility:**
   - Think tank reports explicitly demarcate personal author findings from official institutional policy to preserve analytical independence.
   - *KDI Example:* "끝으로 본 연구의 내용은 저자의 개인적인 의견이며, 본원의 공식적인 견해가 아님을 밝혀 둔다." (KDI Policy Study Series 2015-07, Foreword, p. 4).
   - *BOK Example:* "본 자료의 내용은 한국은행의 공식견해가 아니라 집필자 개인의 견해라는 점을 밝힙니다." (BOK Issue Note No. 2023-5, Cover, p. 1).
3. **Public Intellectual Property Licensing:**
   - Public institute outputs are mandated to carry open government licenses (e.g., Gong-gong Nuri Type 1, 3, or 4: "공공누리 제3유형: 출처표시 + 변경금지").

### 1.3 Government Administrative & Policy Reporting Standards (MOIS & Blue House)

Official Korean government reporting is governed by the **Ministry of the Interior and Safety (행정안전부, MOIS)** *Manual on the Operation and Innovation of Administrative Work (행정업무운영편람)* pursuant to Presidential Decree *Regulations on the Operation and Innovation of Administrative Work (행정업무의 운영 및 혁신에 관한 규정)*:

1. **Standard Page Layout and Unit Sizing:**
   - Paper size is strictly A4 (210 mm x 297 mm, Art. 7, Para. 6).
   - Left/Right margins: 20 mm; Top/Bottom margins: 15 mm. Cell internal padding: 3 pt left/right.
2. **Heading & Subdivision Hierarchy (편람 p. 51):**
   - 8-level nesting standard:
     1. First level: `1.`, `2.`, `3.` (Starts at left base margin)
     2. Second level: `가.`, `나.`, `다.` (Indents 2 spaces / 2타 / 1 character width)
     3. Third level: `1)`, `2)`, `3)` (Indents 4 spaces)
     4. Fourth level: `가)`, `나)`, `다)` (Indents 6 spaces)
     5. Fifth level: `(1)`, `(2)`, `(3)` (Indents 8 spaces)
     6. Sixth level: `(가)`, `(나)`, `(다)` (Indents 10 spaces)
     7. Seventh level: `①`, `②`, `③`
     8. Eighth level: `㉮`, `㉯`, `㉰`
   - *Special Bullet Rule:* If custom bullets are used, avoid fragile glyphs. Recommended sequence: `□` (Major heading), `○` (Subheading), `–` or `▪` (Supporting bullet), `∙` (Minor detail).
3. **The Mandatory "끝" (End) Marking Rule (MOIS Manual pp. 61–62):**
   - After the final word of text: leave 2 spaces (1 full character width) and append `끝.`.
     - Example: `…… 주시기 바랍니다.  끝.`
   - When attachment list (`붙임`) exists: append `끝.` 2 spaces after the last character of the last attachment item line.
     - Example: `2. 승인서식 2부.  끝.`
   - In a table where text reaches the bottom-right cell: write `끝.` on a new line below the table aligned to the left. If empty rows remain in a table, write `이하 빈칸` (below blank) in the next cell and omit `끝.`.
4. **Number and Date Standards (MOIS Manual p. 49):**
   - Digits: Arabic numerals exclusively.
   - Dates: Replace Year/Month/Day words with dots followed by a single space. No leading zeroes.
     - Correct: `2026. 9. 9.` (Must have trailing dot and spaces between units). Incorrect: `2026.09.09` or `2026.9.9`.
   - Time: 24-hour notation separated by colon with leading zero for single digits.
     - Correct: `15:20`, `07:09`. Incorrect: `오후 3시 20분`.
   - Monetary Amounts: Arabic numerals followed by Korean characters in parentheses.
     - Correct: `금113,560원(금일십일만삼천오백육십원)`.
5. **Head-First Architecture (두괄식 원칙) & Conciseness (Blue House Manual p. 4, 36, 40):**
   - "간략한 보고서의 경우 효율상 시작하는 문단에 가장 중요한 내용이 들어가도록 하고, 이어 그것을 뒷받침하는 내용을 배치" (Blue House Manual p. 4).
   - Sentence length: One sentence should not exceed 2 to 3 lines (50–70 syllables per sentence). Avoid convoluted passive constructions ("되어진다" → "한다").

#### Anatomical Page Breakdown:
1. **Page 1: Cover Header & Summary Dashboard**
   - **Header:** Publication category (`COMPANY UPDATE`, `INITIATION`, `SECTOR UPDATE`), publication date (`YYYY. M. D.`), ticker code (`005930 / KS`).
   - **Main Title & Subtitle:** The title identifies the covered corporation, while the subtitle is an assertive thesis (e.g., `AI Agent 시대, 여전히 좁은 메모리 공급의 문`, `주주환원, 기대에서 확신으로`).
   - **Analyst Attribution Box:** Analyst name, formal corporate title, business email, and direct telephone line. Associate analysts listed below.
   - **Core Metric Box (Callout Box):**
     - Investment Rating (`BUY`, `HOLD`, `SELL`).
     - Target Price (목표주가, KRW) and Current Price (현재주가).
     - Expected Upside (상승여력, %).
     - Market Capitalization, 52-Week Range, Outstanding Shares, Foreign Ownership Percentage, Beta.
   - **Executive Summary Block:** Usually titled `WHAT’S THE STORY?` or `Investment Summary`. Structured in 2 to 3 titled sub-blocks: (1) Investment Rating Reason, (2) Valuation & Target Price Calculation, (3) Forward Catalyst / Operational Performance.
   - **Page 1 Table:** Key Financial Metrics Summary (매출액, 영업이익, 순이익, EPS, PER, PBR, ROE, 배당수익률 for Past 2 Years and Forward 3 Years: `2024, 2025, 2026E, 2027E, 2028E`).
2. **Pages 2–3: Body & Evidence Exhibits**
   - **Valuation Derivation Table:** Clear explicit breakdown of valuation methodology (e.g., SOTP, P/B-ROE matrix, Discounted Cash Flow, or P/E multiple vs. peers).
   - **Exhibits & Charts:** P/E and P/B Valuation Bands spanning 5 to 10 years, historical price vs. KOSPI benchmark, operational performance bar charts.
   - **Captions:** Exhibit numbering formats: `<표 1>`, `<그림 1>` or `Table 1`, `Figure 1`. Every single graphic carries a mandatory source line at the bottom: `자료: 회사 자료, Quantiwise, 삼성증권 추정` or `자료: FnGuide, 교보증권 리서치센터`.
3. **Pages 4–5: Financial Statement Appendix**
   - Standard 4-statement summarized layout across 5 columns (Historical 2 Years + Projected 3 Years):
     1. Comprehensive Income Statement (포괄손익계산서)
     2. Statement of Financial Position (재무상태표)
     3. Statement of Cash Flows (현금흐름표)
     4. Key Financial Ratios (주요 투자지표: PER, PBR, EV/EBITDA, ROE, ROA, 부채비율, 순차입금비율).
4. **Final Page: Legal & Compliance Notice**
   - **Analyst Independence Confirmation:** Exact wording certifying no improper influence.
   - **Conflict of Interest Table:** Disclosing 1% equity holdings, liquidity provider agreements, underwriting engagements within the last 12 months, and corporate affiliate relationships.
   - **2-Year Disparity Rate (괴리율) Table & Graphic:** Displays historical date of every target price change, rating, target price, and the disparity percentage against the historical average price and highest/lowest market price.
   - **Rating Distribution Box:** Mandatory percentage breakdown of all coverage ratings over the past 12 months across Buy, Hold, and Sell.
   - **Rating Definitions:** Exact percentage thresholds (e.g., `Buy: Expected 12-month return ≥ +15%`, `Hold: -15% ~ +15%`, `Sell: ≤ -15%`).

1. **The Foreword (발간사):** Signed by the Institute President (원장). 2. **The Executive Summary (요 약):** Positioned immediately preceding Chapter 1. Condenses the entire volume into 1–3 pages for high-level ministerial reading.
3. **Sequential Dual-Language Abstract:** Standard monograph closing includes a full English abstract (`ABSTRACT`) with author affiliations and English title for foreign policy benchmarking.

## 3. Structural Comparison of Korean Professional Reports

The following matrix compares structural components across the major professional report genres in South Korea:

| Structural Dimension | Securities Research Report (증권사 리서치) | Public Think Tank Monograph (KDI/KIET 정책연구) | Institutional Short Note (BOK 이슈노트/KDI Focus) | Government Administrative Report (행안부/공문서) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cover Page Contents** | Company/Sector Name, Ticker, Analyst Contacts, Target Price, Rating, Stock Data Box | Series No., Title, Subtitle, Author Name, Publisher Name & Logo | Series Masthead, Date, Title, Author Affiliation, Executive Summary, Legal Disclaimer | Agency Name, Official Logo (2×2cm), Recipient, Title, Registration Details |
| **Executive Summary Form** | `WHAT’S THE STORY?` or `Investment Summary` (Bullets + Mini Narrative on Page 1) | Formal standalone `요 약` section (1–3 pages) between Foreword and Chapter 1 | Shaded front-page executive box (3–4 paragraphs) beneath title | Integrated introductory background clause (`1. 관련...`) or separate `요약설명문` box |
| **Table of Contents (TOC)** | Generally omitted in short reports (<8p); present in deep Sector Initiations (>20p) | Detailed `목차` (TOC), `표목차` (List of Tables), `그림목차` (List of Figures) | Omitted (Compact 4–18 page format) | Omitted in standard memos; `목차` box added for reports ≥ 5 pages |

## 4. Korean Writing Rules & Conventions

Writing style in professional Korean documents is governed by clear stylistic doctrines distinct from casual Korean prose:

### 4.1 Head-First Structure (두괄식 원칙)
- **The Core Rule:** The conclusion, investment verdict, or policy recommendation must lead every structural unit (document, chapter, section, paragraph).
- **Public & Presidential Guidelines:** The Blue House Report Manual states that decision-makers lack time to read background chronologies. The first sentence of a paragraph must state the definitive finding or decision. Supporting logic and numerical evidence follow.
- **Securities Convention:** Equity research avoids chronological narratives. Page 1 immediately delivers the rating and price target; each section heading states the operational impact (e.g., "3분기 실적 호조에 따른 목표주가 상향" rather than "3분기 실적 분석").

### 4.2 Heading Formulation: Assertive Claim vs. Topic Label
1. **Securities Equity Research (주장형 문장):**
   - Headings are formulated as complete, assertive statements with evaluative verbs or predicates:
     - *Bad (Topic):* `AI 반도체 시장 동향 및 전망`
     - *Good (Claim):* `AI Agent 수요 폭증, 디램 공급 쇼티지는 2028년까지 지속`
     - *Good (Signal):* `삼성물산: 주주환원, 기대에서 확신으로`
2. **Public Policy Monograph (주제형/문제제기형):**
   - Preserves formal scientific taxonomy:
     - `제1장 서 론` → `제1절 연구의 배경 및 목적`
     - `제3장 주택담보대출모형 구축 및 캘리브레이션`
3. **Government Administrative Briefing (목적/행동 중심형):**
   - Combines noun phrases with execution markers:
     - `1. 추진 배경 및 당면 과제`
     - `2. 주요 정책 추진 방향`
     - `3. 향후 조치 계획 (행정사항)`

### 4.3 Sentence Structure, Length, and Style (문체)
1. **Length Control:**
   - Sentences should not exceed 2 to 3 typed lines (approximately 50 to 80 Korean syllables). If a sentence exceeds 100 syllables, it must be split.
2. **Honorifics & Verb Endings (평서체 vs. 존댓말):**
   - **Written Reports, Policy Papers, and Securities Research:** Strictly use the neutral formal written register (해라체 / 평서체 ending in `~다`, `~함`, `~임`):
     - Correct: `...을 유지한다`, `...로 판단된다`, `...할 것으로 예상된다`.
     - Incorrect (Oral/Polite): `...을 유지합니다`, `...로 보여집니다`.
   - **Itemized Government Bulletins (개조식):** Use substantive nominal phrase endings (`명사형 종결`):
     - Correct: `...추진 계획 수립`, `...조치 완료`, `...제도 개선 검토 중`.
   - **External Administrative Communications:** Letters and inter-agency memos use polite official endings (`하십시오체`):
     - Correct: `...참석하여 주시기 바랍니다.`, `...송부하오니 협조하여 주시기 바랍니다.`
3. **Voice (Active vs. Passive):**
   - Government and research manuals strictly prohibit unnatural double-passive translations (이중 피동) derived from English or Japanese:
     - *Prohibited:* `마무리되어진다`, `보여진다`, `생각되어진다`.
     - *Mandated:* `마무리한다`, `판단된다`, `분석된다`.

### 4.4 Number, Date, and Evidence Conventions
1. **Number Formatting:**
   - Arabic numerals are universal (`1,250억원`, `35.6%`).
   - Units of measure must have a space between the number and unit in standard orthography, but financial reports frequently bind units for space (`500,000원`, `100십억원`).
   - Government budget representations avoid confusing thousands (`천원`): specify `만 원` or `억 원` (e.g., `2,134만 5천 원` instead of `21,345천원`).
2. **Date and Time Formatting:**
   - Standard administrative date format requires trailing dots and intermediate spaces:
     - `2026. 9. 9.` (Period after Year, Month, Day, with space).
   - Abbreviated year forms take an apostrophe before the two digits: `’26년`.
   - Time uses colon without spaces: `14:00`, `09:30`.
3. **Table & Chart Source Line Conventions:**
   - Every figure and table must have an unambiguous attribution line directly below the frame:
     - Securities: `자료: 삼성증권 추정`, `자료: Company data, FnGuide, 당사 리서치센터`.
     - Academic/Public: `자료: 통계청, ｢가계금융⋅복지조사｣, 각 연도.`, `출처: 한국은행 경제통계시스템(ECOS)`.
   - In government documents, references to external studies must state the publishing ministry or institution, author, and year in brackets.
