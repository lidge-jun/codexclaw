Only one framework's loop is described specifically enough to classify

Open Deep Research is an orchestrator-worker loop: a clarification step, a research brief, a supervisor that spawns parallel researcher sub-agents each holding isolated context, a compression stage, and a dedicated final report writer. Model roles are split across summarization, research, compression, and report generation.

SkyworkAI DeepResearchAgent was described as hierarchical with a top-level planner, which sounds orchestrator-worker but is not specific enough to distinguish from recursive decomposition. The rest carry no architectural description at all.

Citation handling is your decision driver and the weakest-evidenced column

GPT Researcher was described as producing "cited reports", but nothing describes the mechanism. The distinction that matters is whether source URLs are carried through the pipeline as structured data alongside retrieved text, or re-emitted by the model at write time. A model that regenerates URLs from context at report time will invent them, which is exactly the failure a citation-heavy bot cannot tolerate.

This is also why the orchestrator-worker shape is the right target regardless of which project you pick. A separate compression stage and a separate report writer give you two places to keep (claim, source_url) paired as data.
