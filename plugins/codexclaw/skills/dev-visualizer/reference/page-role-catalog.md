# Page roles, not repeated panels

The original `assets/paged-report.html` and `assets/consulting-ko.css` are a
coherent example, not a copy of any consultancy's brand. Reuse hierarchy and
reasoning, not a report's proprietary type, logos, illustrations or page artwork.

| Role | Reader task | Composition | Failure to catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cover | Understand scope and governing answer | One dominant claim, short qualification, issuer/date/scope | Author-process narrative or invented issuer authority |
| Contents | Navigate the argument | Claim headings, reliable page numbers | Stale or guessed references |
| Summary | Decide without reading the body | Answer plus evidence, trade-off, requested action and limits | Stronger causal claim than the evidence supports |
| Narrative | Understand a mechanism or alternative | Bounded prose measure; headings carry progression | A tour of the author's investigation |
| Exhibit | Compare or test the claim | Takeaway title, marks/table, denominator, method and source | Unreadable labels, decorative data, duplicate table and chart |
| Decision | Act with clear trade-offs | Options, chosen action, owner, sequence and success criterion | A recommendation presented as completed execution |
| Appendix | Verify and reuse evidence | Definitions, detailed source locators, assumptions | Caveats hidden only here when they qualify the summary |

A brief can combine roles on one page. A long table can span pages and repeat its
header. Do not impose a chapter break on every section. Use a full-width exhibit
when comparison needs it, not merely to occupy blank space. Covers, openers and
summary pages may use purposeful whitespace; excessive space splitting related
content is still a defect. Inspect role and geometry together.

House-style defaults: 10.5pt Korean body, about 1.65 leading, 8.5pt source notes,
17pt section headings, 28pt cover claim, one accent, hairline table rules. These
are starting points for actual page review, not facts about consulting firms.
Two-line headings are allowed. Prefer short paragraphs over shrinking whole pages.
Keep word-based Korean wrapping, mixed-script symbols and actual numeric columns
under visual review. Local font manifest roles replace the body/heading families.

Big numbers, tinted summaries and attributed commentary can be useful when they
carry specific evidence. Do not add them by reflex or prohibit them universally.
The quiet profile uses tables and prose for its summary; a different justified
profile can use another arrangement while retaining evidence and accessibility.
